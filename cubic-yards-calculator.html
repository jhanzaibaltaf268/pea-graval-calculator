Cubic Yards Calculator

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, the space of a cube 3 feet on each side. To find cubic yards, multiply length x width x depth in feet and divide by 27, or use length x width (ft) x depth (in) / 324. How to calculate cubic yards

  1. Measure length, width and depth.
  2. Convert everything to feet. Divide inches by 12 and centimetres by 30.48.
  3. Multiply length x width x depth to get cubic feet.
  4. Divide by 27 to get cubic yards.
  5. Add 5 to 10% for waste, settling and uneven ground.
cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (ft) ÷ 27

Example: a 20 x 10 ft bed, 3 inches deep. Depth is 0.25 ft. Volume is 20 x 10 x 0.25 = 50 cubic feet, which is 1.85 cubic yards.

How much is a cubic yard?

One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, 46,656 cubic inches or 0.765 cubic metres. It is about the size of a washing machine, or a pile roughly 3 feet wide, 3 feet deep and 3 feet tall.

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

There are 27 cubic feet in a cubic yard (3 feet x 3 feet x 3 feet).

How much area does a cubic yard cover?

It depends on the depth: 324 square feet at 1 inch, 162 at 2 inches, 108 at 3 inches, 81 at 4 inches and 54 at 6 inches.

How many yards are in a ton of gravel?

About 0.71 cubic yards at 1.4 tons per cubic yard. Divide the tons by the density to get cubic yards.

How many cubic yards fit in a truck bed or wheelbarrow?

A full-size pickup bed holds roughly 1.5 to 2 cubic yards of loose, light material such as mulch when filled level, but weight is often the limit: a cubic yard of gravel weighs about 2,800 lb, which is more than many pickups should carry. A 6 cubic foot wheelbarrow takes 4.5 loads per cubic yard, and a 3 cubic foot one takes 9.

Cubic yards in construction and landscaping

Suppliers sell bulk soil, mulch, gravel, sand and concrete by the cubic yard, so this is the number to know before you order. Ready-mix concrete is sold in whole or partial cubic yards, and delivery trucks usually have a minimum load. Round up to the next quarter or half yard to avoid running short.

Cubic yards vs square yards vs square feet

Cubic yards measure volume (a three-dimensional amount), while square yards and square feet measure area. You cannot convert between them without a depth. To go from square feet to cubic yards, see the Square Feet to Cubic Yards Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert square feet to cubic yards?

Multiply square feet by the depth in inches and divide by 324.

How much does a cubic yard of gravel weigh?

About 1.4 tons, or 2,800 lb.

Is a cubic yard the same for every material?

The volume is the same, but the weight is not. A cubic yard of mulch weighs a fraction of a cubic yard of gravel.

How many wheelbarrow loads are in a cubic yard?

About 4.5 loads for a 6 cubic foot wheelbarrow.

How many cubic yards do I need for a patio?

A 10 x 12 ft patio at 3 inches deep needs 1.11 cubic yards.

Method and sources

Volume = length × width × depth, converted at 27 cubic feet per cubic yard. Densities are typical planning figures.
