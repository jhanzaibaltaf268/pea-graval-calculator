How this is calculated

The calculator multiplies driveway area (length × width) by depth to get cubic feet, converts to cubic yards, then multiplies by your material's density to estimate weight in tons.

cubic yards = (length × width × depth) ÷ 27
tons = cubic yards × material density

For long driveways, order in bulk by the ton — most suppliers deliver by dump truck and price per ton.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should gravel be on a driveway?

A typical gravel driveway uses 4–6 inches of base material (crushed stone or road base), sometimes with a thinner 1–2 inch decorative top layer.

How many tons of gravel do I need for a driveway?

It depends on your dimensions. A 50 ft × 12 ft driveway at 4 inches deep needs roughly 8.2 cubic yards, or about 12 tons of crushed stone. Use the calculator above for your exact numbers.

What's the best gravel for a driveway base?

Crushed stone or road base (crush and run) compacts well and is the standard structural layer. Pea gravel or decorative gravel is often added as a thinner top layer for looks.
