What is river rock?

River rock is smooth, rounded stone shaped by moving water, usually between about 1 and 5 inches, though suppliers sell it in many size ranges and colors. It is used for decoration and drainage rather than as a structural base, because rounded stones do not lock together the way angular crushed stone does. For small, rounded stone under 3/8 inch, see the Pea Gravel Calculator.

Worked examples

Example 1: dry creek bed, 3 ft wide x 20 ft long at 4 inches

Area is 60 sq ft. Volume is 60 x 4 / 324 = 0.74 cubic yards, or 1.0 ton. With 10% extra, order 0.81 cubic yards (1.1 tons).

Example 2: tree ring, 6 ft diameter at 3 inches

Area is 3.14 x 3 x 3 = 28.3 sq ft, but subtract the trunk area if the ring has a hole. Volume is 28.3 x 3 / 324 = 0.26 cubic yards, or 0.35 tons. With 10% extra, 0.29 cubic yards (0.39 tons).

Example 3: path, 3 ft wide x 25 ft long at 3 inches

Area is 75 sq ft. Volume is 75 x 3 / 324 = 0.69 cubic yards, or 0.94 tons. With 10% extra, 0.76 cubic yards (1.03 tons).

How much does river rock weigh?

River rock density and the yards-to-tons conversion

River rock typically weighs about 1.3 to 1.5 tons per cubic yard (2,600 to 3,000 lb), depending on the stone, size and moisture. The calculator uses 1.35. To convert: tons = cubic yards x 1.35, and cubic yards = tons / 1.35. Smaller stones pack tighter and weigh slightly more per yard than large ones. Different rock types, such as lava rock, are much lighter, so check the types of gravel guide to choose the right material before you calculate.

Ordering tips and how much extra to buy

Add 10% to your calculated quantity, round up to the next half cubic yard, and compare bulk against bags: bags suit up to about half a cubic yard, and bulk delivery is usually cheaper above that. Ask the supplier for a size range rather than a single size, since river rock varies, and buy everything for the project in one go so the color and size match. Frequently asked questions

How much river rock do I need?

Multiply area by depth and divide by 324 to get cubic yards. For 100 sq ft at 3 inches, you need 0.93 cubic yards, about 1.25 tons.

How many tons of river rock are in a cubic yard?

About 1.35 tons, though it can range from about 1.3 to 1.5.

What depth should river rock be?

Usually 2 to 3 inches for beds and paths, and 4 to 6 inches for a dry creek bed.

What size river rock is best for a dry creek bed?

A mix of sizes works best: larger 3 to 5 inch stones along the edges and in the flow line, with smaller rock between them. Use fabric underneath.

Is river rock more expensive than pea gravel?

Usually yes, because it is selected, washed and sorted for appearance. Get quotes per ton delivered.

Method and sources

Volume = length x width x depth, converted at 27 cubic feet per cubic yard. Weight = volume x density. Densities are typical planning figures; confirm with your supplier.
