How much gravel for a French drain?

Take the volume of the trench and subtract the pipe. The trench is a long box, so volume is length x width x depth. Then divide by 27 for cubic yards.

cubic feet = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (ft) − pipe volume
cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27

Example: a 50 ft trench, 12 inches wide and 18 inches deep with a 4 inch pipe. Trench: 50 x 1 x 1.5 = 75 cubic feet. Pipe: 3.14 x (4/24)² x 50 = about 4.4 cubic feet. Gravel: 70.6 cubic feet, which is 2.6 cubic yards, about 3.5 tons at 1.35.

What size drain rock for a French drain?

Clean, washed stone between about 3/4 inch and 1.5 inches drains best. Clean stone has no fines, so water passes through. Common choices are #57 stone and clean 3/4 inch drain rock. Avoid stone with fines or crusher run, because the dust can clog the drain. Pea gravel also works but is less common.

Filter fabric

Filter fabric lines the trench so soil does not wash into the stone. The estimate above assumes the fabric covers the bottom and both sides plus a 1 ft overlap on top. Real quantities depend on how you wrap it, so buy a little extra.

French drain gravel FAQ

How much gravel do I need for a French drain?

Multiply trench length, width and depth in feet, subtract the pipe volume, and divide by 27 to get cubic yards.

What size gravel for a French drain?

Clean washed stone, about 3/4 to 1.5 inches, such as #57 stone.

How many tons is a cubic yard of drain rock?

About 1.3 to 1.4 tons for clean stone.

Do I need filter fabric?

Most installs wrap the trench in fabric to keep soil out of the stone.

Is this calculator exact?

No. It is a planning estimate. Confirm the density and quantity with your supplier.
