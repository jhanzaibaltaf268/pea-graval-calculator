How to calculate how much sand you need

Multiply length by width in feet and by depth in inches, then divide by 324. That is cubic yards. Multiply by the weight per cubic yard to get tons.

cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 324
tons = cubic yards × tons per cubic yard

Example: a 10 x 10 ft area, 2 inches deep, is 100 x 2 ÷ 324 = 0.62 cubic yards. At 1.35 tons per cubic yard that is about 0.83 tons.

How much does a cubic yard of sand weigh?

Dry sand weighs about 1.3 to 1.4 tons per cubic yard (roughly 2,700 lb). Damp sand weighs about 1.5 tons, and soaked sand can reach 1.7 tons or more. Sand is often delivered damp, so plan on the damp figure unless your supplier says otherwise.

Sandbox sand calculator

Most sandboxes want 6 to 8 inches of play sand so children cannot dig down to the base. A 4 x 4 ft sandbox at 6 inches deep needs 16 x 6 ÷ 324 = 0.30 cubic yards, about 0.4 tons of dry sand. Enter 4, 4 and 6 above to check. Play sand is also sold in 50 lb bags: 0.30 cubic yards is about 800 lb, or roughly 16 bags at 50 lb each.

Mason sand and paver sand

Mason sand is fine and clean, used for bedding pavers and mixing mortar. Paver bedding sand is usually laid about 1 inch deep, so a 10 x 12 ft patio needs 120 x 1 ÷ 324 = 0.37 cubic yards. For the stone base under the sand, use the paver base and sand calculator.

Arena sand calculator

Riding arenas are commonly built with a few inches of sand over a compacted base. Enter your arena length and width in feet, use the depth in inches (often 3 to 4), and choose arena sand. A 60 x 120 ft arena at 3 inches is 7,200 x 3 ÷ 324 = 66.7 cubic yards, roughly 90 to 100 tons. Check the depth and sand type with your arena builder.

Moisture changes the weight, not the volume. One cubic yard is about 1.35 tons dry, 1.5 tons damp and 1.7 tons wet in this calculator. Damp sand is about 11% heavier than dry sand and wet sand about 26% heavier, so a quote in tons depends on how wet the pile is.

Sand calculator FAQ

How much sand do I need?

Multiply length by width in feet and by depth in inches, then divide by 324 for cubic yards. Multiply by 1.35 to 1.5 for tons.

How much does a cubic yard of sand weigh?

About 1.35 tons (2,700 lb) when dry and about 1.5 tons when damp.

How much sand goes in a sandbox?

Plan on 6 to 8 inches. A 4 x 4 ft sandbox at 6 inches needs about 0.3 cubic yards.

How much sand do I need under pavers?

Bedding sand is usually about 1 inch deep, which is about 0.37 cubic yards for 120 square feet.

Is this calculator exact?

No. It is a planning estimate. Confirm the density and quantity with your supplier.

Does damp sand weigh more than dry sand?

Yes. This calculator uses about 1.35 tons per cubic yard for dry sand, 1.5 for damp sand and 1.7 for wet sand, so damp sand is about 11% heavier than dry and wet sand about 26% heavier. Sand delivered from an outdoor pile is often damp.

How much sand do I need for a 6 × 6 ft sandbox that is 8 inches deep?

About 0.89 cubic yards, or roughly 1.33 tons of damp sand at 1.5 tons per cubic yard. With 10% extra, order about 0.98 cubic yards or 1.47 tons.
