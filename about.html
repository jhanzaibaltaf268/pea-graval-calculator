About GravelCalc

GravelCalc is a set of free calculators that turn the size of a job into the amount of gravel, sand, stone or soil to order, in cubic yards or cubic metres, tons or kilograms.

Last updated November 2, 2026

What the site does

Every calculator takes a length, width and depth, works out the volume, and converts it to weight using a typical bulk density for the material you choose. You can add an allowance for compaction and waste, and copy the result into a message to your supplier. The English pages work in feet, inches and cubic yards, or metres and centimetres. The German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Dutch and Italian pages work in metric units.

How the numbers are calculated

cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 324
cubic metres = length (m) × width (m) × depth (cm) ÷ 100
weight = volume × material density

The maths runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server, and the site has no accounts.

Where the density figures come from

Densities are planning figures for loose, delivered material, for example about 1.4 tons per cubic yard for crushed stone or 1.5 to 1.8 tons per cubic metre for gravel. Real values vary with rock type, grain size, moisture and compaction, so the pages label them as estimates and tell you to confirm quantity and density with your supplier before you order. The site does not publish prices, because they depend on region and delivery distance.

Corrections

If a figure looks wrong for your region, or a calculator misbehaves, tell us which page, what you entered and what you expected. Corrections are checked and the page’s “last updated” date changes when its content does.
