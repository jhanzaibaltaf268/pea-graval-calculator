Square Feet to Cubic Yards Calculator

Cubic yards = square feet x depth in inches / 324. For example, 100 square feet at 3 inches deep is 100 x 3 / 324 = 0.93 cubic yards.

You cannot convert square feet to cubic yards without a depth, because square feet measure area and cubic yards measure volume. How to convert square feet to cubic yards

  1. Find the area in square feet (length x width).
  2. Choose the depth in inches.
  3. Multiply area x depth in inches, then divide by 324.
  4. Add 5 to 10% if you are ordering material.

Square feet to cubic yards formula

cubic yards = square feet × depth (in) ÷ 324

If your depth is in feet, use: cubic yards = square feet × depth (ft) ÷ 27.

Where the number 324 comes from

One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet. Depth in inches has to be turned into feet by dividing by 12. So 27 × 12 = 324: area (sq ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 ÷ 27 simplifies to area × depth ÷ 324. The two forms below are the same formula written two ways.

  • Cubic yards = sq ft × (depth in inches ÷ 12) ÷ 27
  • Cubic yards = sq ft × depth in inches ÷ 324

Why a depth is required

Square feet is a flat surface, with no thickness. A 100 square foot patio covered 1 inch deep and 100 square feet covered 12 inches deep are very different amounts of material: 0.31 cubic yards versus 3.7 cubic yards. Always decide the depth first.

Worked examples

Example 1: 150 sq ft at 2 inches (mulch or gravel)

150 × 2 ÷ 324 = 0.93 cubic yards. With 10% extra, 1.02 cubic yards.

Example 2: 400 sq ft lawn at 4 inches (topsoil)

400 × 4 ÷ 324 = 4.94 cubic yards. With 10% extra, 5.43 cubic yards.

How many cubic yards of gravel or soil does my area need?

Once you have cubic yards, multiply by the material's density to get tons: about 1.4 tons per cubic yard for gravel and crushed stone, 1.1 to 1.4 for topsoil, and 0.4 to 0.6 for mulch. Use the Pea Gravel Calculator for stone and the Cubic Yards Calculator for other shapes.

Common uses

  • Topsoil and compost for lawns and gardens
  • Mulch and bark for beds
  • Gravel, sand and crushed stone for paths, patios and driveways
  • Concrete for slabs
  • Asphalt for patching

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the depth. Square feet alone cannot give you cubic yards.
  • Mixing units. Use inches with 324, or feet with 27. Do not mix them.
  • Confusing square yards with cubic yards. Square yards are area, cubic yards are volume.
  • Forgetting waste. Add 5 to 10% for uneven ground and settling.
  • Using volume when you need weight. Suppliers often sell by the ton; convert using density.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert square feet to cubic yards?

Multiply square feet by depth in inches and divide by 324.

How many square feet does a cubic yard cover?

324 square feet at 1 inch, 162 at 2 inches, 108 at 3 inches, 81 at 4 inches.

Do I need cubic feet before I can find cubic yards?

No. The 324 formula goes directly from square feet and inches to cubic yards. If you already have cubic feet, divide by 27.

What is the difference between cubic yards and square yards?

A square yard is an area of 9 square feet; a cubic yard is a volume of 27 cubic feet.

How do I convert cubic yards to square feet?

Multiply cubic yards by 324 and divide by the depth in inches. For example, 2 cubic yards at 3 inches covers 2 × 324 ÷ 3 = 216 square feet.

Method and sources
