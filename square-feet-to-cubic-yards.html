How this is calculated

Convert depth to feet, multiply by your area to get cubic feet, then divide by 27 to get cubic yards.

cubic feet = area (sq ft) × (depth ÷ 12)
cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert square feet to cubic yards?

Multiply your area in square feet by your depth in feet (inches ÷ 12) to get cubic feet, then divide by 27. Example: 300 sq ft at 3 inches deep = 300 × 0.25 ÷ 27 ≈ 2.78 cubic yards.

How many square feet does a cubic yard cover?

One cubic yard covers about 324 square feet at 1 inch deep, 162 square feet at 2 inches, or 108 square feet at 3 inches — see the table above for more depths.
